Our Story

Made for Pets, Inspired by Their Happiness

Pawsome & Co. began in 2014 in a small Portland storefront, started by two lifelong pet owners who were tired of choosing between quality and affordability for their own dogs and cats

Today we serve thousands of pet families with carefully sourced food, accessories, and grooming services — but the mission hasn't changed: make it easier to give every pet a happy, healthy life.
